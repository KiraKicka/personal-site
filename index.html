§ 00.1 — abstract

I design, simulate and qualify hardware that has to survive vacuum, cold and launch. For four years I led the thermo-mechanical architecture of MiLi, a miniaturised LiDAR built to read the atmosphere of Mars.

Trained as a welding engineer in Moscow, detoured through deep-tech investment, finished a PhD at Politecnico di Milano. Today: process engineering at Technoprobe, and a nanosatellite startup on the side.
