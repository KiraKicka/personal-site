§ 03.1 — instrument

MiLi

A miniaturised LiDAR for sounding the atmosphere of Mars. Six kilograms, under fifteen watts, and a laser that has to stay at the right temperature while everything around it doesn’t.

§ 03.2 — method four things, in order
i.

Draw it

Optical bench, emitter and detector mounts, TEC brackets. Models and GD&T drawings for the shop.

ii.

Keep it cool

A TEC loop for the NIR emitter: characterisation, PID tuning, electro-thermal verification across the range.

iii.

Prove it

Quasi-static, modal and thermoelastic FEA in ANSYS — then the same questions asked again of real hardware.

iv.

Break it, nearly

150+ hours of thermal vacuum, five vibration campaigns, functional tests before and after each.
