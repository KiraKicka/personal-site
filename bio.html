§ 04 Bio b. 1998, Rostov-on-Don

bio

The longer version, without the bullet points.

I was born in Rostov-on-Don in 1998 and studied in Moscow, at Bauman, where I trained as a welding engineer. My thesis was about arc-welding aluminium pressure vessels for aerospace — thermal deformation, hot cracks, and the fixtures that hold it all still.

Then I spent two years on the other side of the table, as a technology investment analyst at Moscow Partners: reading deep-tech companies for a living and learning to explain engineering to people who sign the cheques.

In 2022 I moved to Italy for a PhD at Politecnico di Milano, in the MetroSpace Lab on the Lecco campus, and spent four years on MiLi — with a season at INTA in Madrid, aligning optics in a cleanroom.

I still live in Lecco, between the lake and the mountains. I work as a process engineer at Technoprobe and co-founded SATELLARIO, a nanosatellite startup that listens for GNSS jamming.
